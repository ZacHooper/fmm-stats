- [Player analysis methods](player-analysis-methods.md) — repeatable recipes for squad/prospect questions + the traps that invert answers. **The published mart holds ONE snapshot** — per-snapshot attribute history is in the FULL store's `staging.player_attributes` (~482k rows). Only **8 attributes are exact save-wide** (Pace, Strength, Stamina, Technique, Aggression, Leadership, Agility, Teamwork); the other 15 are a frozen linear decode of the player's own byte (~93% within ±1) — Crossing takes no Technique input, so cross-attribute questions are not circular on estimated data. What works: phase-splitting with a same-opponent control, **age-adjusted percentiles** (ranks a 17yo against 17yos — flipped two verdicts), and forecasting an attribute from **its own current value + age**, tracked longitudinally (R²=0.85 for Crossing 4yrs out) — NOT from a different attribute like Technique, which adds +0.000 R² once current value is known (a RETRACTED earlier method that projected off mature Technique peers overstated growth ~3×, since it was really reading survivorship). Shipped as `mart.attribute_forecast` / `site/api/forecast.json` / the Development tab. Growth is front-loaded by age (median 8 pts/yr at 16 falling to ~1 at 24); **Agility and Technique never move (confirmed on real values)**, and the decode separately compresses Movement/Positioning/Aerial 8–24× so those read as unmodelled rather than frozen. What does NOT work at this sample size: pairing/WOWY synergy (0 of 197 pairs cleared 2 SE; underpowered ~3×) and individual uplift (confounded by loan churn — the with/without split is really a phase split). Small real-attribute samples can INVERT a sign, not just widen it.
- [Scouting attribute reads](scouting-attribute-reads.md) — an attribute is only a weakness against its COUNTERPART: Movement is the attacker's side of the duel, **Positioning is the defender's answer** (role_weights scores movement at baseline for a CB and positioning at baseline for every forward). Two briefings led with an opponent back line's Movement — meaningless. Decoded attributes ARE accurate enough to quote as-is (~63% exact / ~93% within ±1); the 8–24× "compression" in [[player-analysis-methods]] is about year-over-year GROWTH and must not be imported into a point-in-time scouting read. Real cautions are football ones: check individual defenders (a back four averaging 12 can contain an 8), and one weak attribute doesn't decide a duel.
- [Scoring and shot quality](scoring-and-shot-quality.md) — what drives Frem's goals: **shots ON TARGET (r=+0.72), not volume** — we take ~8 a game regardless and goals triple across SOT-rate quartiles while shot count stays flat. The in-game lever is the `Work Into Box` team instruction, not team selection. Second real finding: **playing a centre-forward is worth ~0.66 goals/game** (0.78 strikerless vs 1.44, monotonic in how many are fielded). Also records four attractive hypotheses that FAILED testing (aerial dominance r=+0.03; "crosses need a target" — no difference; opponent ball-winning; the two-best-MCs pairing at 1.3 SE) so they are not re-derived.
- [Attribute → stat correlations](attribute-stat-correlations.md) — what a player's attributes make him actually DO, per positional unit, over 104 player-seasons. Run it with `scripts/attribute_stat_correlations.py`; method + traps in the **attribute-profiles** skill. Headlines: interceptions are Positioning (.51) + Tackling (.49) and **forwards physically cannot supply them** (0.5–1.4 per 90 vs 6–7 for centre-backs), so press intensity is an instruction not a selection; **Shooting drives shots, shots on target and goals** (.82 on SOT within Attack), making it the forward attribute; **nothing predicts tackle success % or cross completion %**; and `SOT %` is almost attribute-free while `shots on target` is attribute-dominated — who shoots is a player, how well the team shoots is `Work Into Box`. Two structural traps: correlate WITHIN unit (a whole-squad correlation just rediscovers position) and use player-SEASONS with contemporaneous attributes (career totals + latest snapshot flipped Aggression-on-interceptions from +0.27 to −0.31 and put a wrong recommendation into a team talk).
