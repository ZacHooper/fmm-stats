23/24 was the season Frem won the NordicBet Liga and reached the 3F Superliga. It was the club's third title in three seasons. These are the 21 players who made ten or more competitive appearances that year, and where they are now.

The season

The spine was built cheaply in one summer. In July and August 2023 Frem took three players from Akademisk Boldklub: Anton Pedersen (£16,000), Frederik Ellegaard (£8,000) and Rasmus Møller (£30,000). All three became regulars. Two teenagers arrived on frees from FC Nordsjælland, Gustav Ullits and Frederik Karlsen, and both are still at the club five years later.

The goals came from Adam Jakobsen, a Frem youth product who had left for Vejle in 2018 and came back on a free. He scored hat-tricks against Helsingør, Nykøbing and Hvidovre in five weeks of autumn. In January Anosike Ementa joined from AaB and scored 11 with 5 assists in 15 games. Pedersen added 9 goals from centre-back.

The cup went badly. Frem lost 2–1 away to Frederiksberg Alliancen 2000 in the first round of the Sydbank Pokalen on 9 August, which ties for the shortest cup run of the career. The worst league result came near the end of the season, a 6–2 loss at Aalborg on 19 May. It is still in the record book (see the right-hand list).

Adam Jakobsen

STThird spell at the club · back since July 2028
23/24 · 29 apps · 30 G · 2 A · 8.34

He holds four of the club's single-season records, all set in this one season.

FC Nordsjælland paid £1.6m for him in January 2025. He scored 16 for them in 26/27, the best season of his time there. In July 2028 Frem paid £2.4m to bring him back. That is £800,000 more than they sold him for, and it gives him a third spell at the club. He has played 10 games so far this season.
