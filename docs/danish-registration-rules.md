# Danish League Registration Rules

Scope: Superliga, 1. Division, 2. Division, 3. Division ("Herre-DM").

Every claim below carries an inline reference. Reference keys resolve in the
**References** section at the end.

Sections 1–4 are confirmed against the rulebook text. Section 5 (loan caps)
remains unresolved — see the note there.

---

## 1. The A-list and B-list system

To be eligible, every player used on the pitch must be registered on either
spillerliste A or spillerliste B **and approved by the administrator**. [TR §14.1]

### A-list — max 25 players (all four divisions)

- Hard cap of **25 players**, submitted via KlubOffice before the season starts.
  [TR §14.1; TR §14.2]
- **Changeable only within the two annual transfer windows.** [TR §14.1]
- **Superliga and 1. Division only:** to get all 25 approved, the A-list must
  include at least **8 "Home Grown" players**: [TR §14.1]
  - **At least 4 trained at the club itself.** [TR §14.1]
  - **The remaining up to 4 trained at another Danish club.** [TR §14.1]
  - The two categories sum to 8 — the club-trained 4 count *toward* the 8.
    [TR §14.1 — "minimum 8 ... hvoraf mindst 4 ... og de øvrige op til 4"]
- **Penalty:** the A-list is reduced by the number of missing homegrown players.
  Six homegrown means a 23-man A-list. No fine, no points deduction. [TR §14.1]
- **2. and 3. Division:** the 25 cap applies, with **no homegrown requirement**.
  [TR §14.2]

### B-list — unlimited

- Unlimited number of players who were **under 21 at the last new year before
  the tournament year**. [TR §14.1; TR §14.2]
- Submitted before the season starts, but **changeable continuously during the
  season**, provided the players were registered at the club by the close of the
  most recent transfer window. [TR §14.1]
- B-list eligible players *can* go on the A-list, but DBU advises against it —
  the B-list preserves A-list capacity. [KO, "Validering af alder"]

## 2. Home Grown definition

A player is Home Grown at a club if he has been **eligible to play
(spilleberettiget) at that club for a minimum of 36 months in total**, from the
start of the season in which he turns 15 to the end of the season in which he
turns 21. [TR §14.1]

- **36 months cumulative**, not three consecutive seasons. [TR §14.1 — "minimum
  36 måneder (totalt set)"]
- The window explicitly ends at the **end of the age-21 season** — seven seasons
  of runway. [TR §14.1]
- Nationality is not mentioned as a criterion. [TR §14.1]
- Same definition applies to the "another Danish club" category. [TR §14.1 —
  "samme definition"]
- Clubs self-declare by ticking the category in KlubOffice. [KO, step 5]

### Loans and homegrown accrual

A player must be a member of the club whose team he plays for, and **this applies
to loaned players too**. [TR §15.2] A player cannot play for one club here while
simultaneously playing for another club in other official competitions. [TR §15.3]
So a loanee is a member of, and registered with, the host club for the loan period.

The rulebook doesn't state the consequence for the 36-month clock. That loan time
accrues to the host rather than the parent club is **my inference** from §15.2,
not a sourced rule. [INFERENCE]

## 3. Registration periods and windows

- **A-list:** before season start, then only within the two annual transfer
  windows. [TR §14.1]
- **B-list:** before season start, then continuously. [TR §14.1]
- **Winter practice:** after the winter window closes the club enters only
  **arrivals and departures**, not a full re-submission. [KO, "Overgangsperioder
  — Vinter"]
- **Window dates:** not stated in the rulebook; KlubOffice points to FIFA's
  transfer window register. [KO, "Overgangsperioder"; FIFA-TMS]
- **Unemployed-player exception:** a club may add a player outside the windows
  under the "arbejdsløs spiller" dispensation. This is the only A-list exception.
  [KO, "Tilføjelse af arbejdsløse spillere", citing CIRK-32 §3.2.A]
- **Matchday additions** to the B-list (e.g. U19 injury cover) take effect
  immediately. [KO, "Tilføjelse af en spiller på kampdag"]

**Correction to an earlier claim in this thread:** I previously described a second
registration lock at the grundspil/slutspil split. That was an over-reading of a
KlubOffice UI mechanic [KO, "Tilgå A- og B-spillerlister"]; the governing rule is
that A-list changes happen only in transfer windows [TR §14.1].

## 4. Matchday eligibility

- **Minimum age 16** on matchday. [TR §13.2]
- **Matchday squad:** 11 starters plus up to **9 substitutes**, one of whom is
  the reserve keeper. [TR §22.3] Five substitutions across a maximum of three
  stoppages. [TR §22.2]
- **Same-day rule:** a player who has played another competitive match the same
  matchday is ineligible; Friday/Saturday/Sunday counts as one matchday. [TR §17.1]
- **U21 and reserve-keeper exemption:** a substitute who was under 21 at the last
  new year, or the reserve keeper, is treated as if he hadn't played for the
  Herre-DM team, and may play the same matchday for a lower-ranked team of the
  club. Capped at **3 such players** on the second senior team's teamsheet.
  [TR §22.4; exemption from §17.1 also stated at TR §22.3]
- **Penalty for fielding an ineligible player:** normally a forfeit if the team
  won or drew. [TR §39.2]

§22.4 is the real youth-development lever: a 20-year-old can sit on the senior
bench and still play for the reserves the same weekend. [TR §22.4]

## 5. Loans — UNRESOLVED

**No loan cap appears anywhere in the Turneringsregler.** The full 2025-26
document was read; §§12–17 cover eligibility and registration and set no limit on
loaned players in or out. [TR §§12.1–17.4]

A dedicated circular exists — **Cirkulære nr. 31 af 2022, "Leje af spillere i
Herre-DM"** — which is where any domestic loan rules would live. [DF-LR] Its PDF
is hosted on `cms.divisionsforeningen.dk`, which blocks automated fetching, and
no usable excerpt surfaced in search. **The domestic loan position is therefore
unverified.** [NOT RETRIEVED] The circular is downloadable by hand from [DF-LR].

Confirmed from the Turneringsregler:

- A loaned player must be a member of the host club. [TR §15.2]
- No dual registration across clubs in official competitions. [TR §15.3]

FIFA's international loan rules (in force July 2022) also bind Danish clubs:

- **6 international loans in and 6 out** per club from 2024-25, phased down from
  8 via 7. [FIFA-2022]
- Max **3 professionals** loaned out to, or in from, any single club. [FIFA-2022]
- **Sub-loaning** prohibited. [FIFA-2022]
- Duration: minimum two transfer windows, maximum one year. [FIFA-2022]
- **Exempt:** homegrown players and players under 21. [FIFA-2022]

Note the Turneringsregler make FIFA rules binding via a separate circular.
[CIRK-34, per DF-LR listing — circular text not retrieved]

## 7. How Football Manager models this

From a sortitoutsi forum thread by a user building a custom Scandinavian league
in the FM editor. These are **editor fields described by a forum poster**, not
the shipped Danish ruleset and not an official SI source. [SOTS]

- Max squad size; loan players in squad (cap, e.g. 3); U23 and U19 minimums;
  homegrown-at-club minimums for both squad and match squad; non-EU limit in the
  playing XI. [SOTS]

Quirks:

- FM's editor labels club-homegrown as **"3 years between 0 and 21"** — a wider
  age window than the real rule. [SOTS, vs TR §14.1]
- FM's unlimited-U21 behaviour is a decent match for the real B-list.
  [OBSERVATION]
- The AI handles tight registration rules badly — in that thread AI clubs kept
  squads at the minimum and omitted their best players; the poster fixed it by
  loosening the rules. [SOTS]

**FMM22:** the mobile game simplifies registration heavily; expect a squad-size
cap without homegrown enforcement. [UNVERIFIED — check the competition rules
screen in your save.]
